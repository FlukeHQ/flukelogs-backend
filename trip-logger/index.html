What this app records

Your location is recorded only between Start Trip and End Trip. Nothing runs at any other time. End the trip before you leave the boat. If you forget, it keeps recording, including your drive home. Guests see your boat's position on a delay, rounded to about a kilometre; they never see exactly where you are.
